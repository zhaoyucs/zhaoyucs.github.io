I am a postdoctoral researcher at Institute of Computing and Intelligence, Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen), working with Prof. [Min Zhang](https://zhangmin-nlp-ai.github.io/) and Prof. [Meishan Zhang](https://zhangmeishan.github.io/). I am also working as a researcher at Tianjin Artificial Intelligence Innovation Center, with senior researcher [Erwei Yin](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Erwei-Yin?ev=hdr_xprf). Previously, I was a visiting researcher at National University of Singapore, where I worked with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua. I graduated as Ph.D from Tianjin University.


Research
======
My research interests lie in Spatial Understanding with technologies of NLP, CV, and the intersection of both (i.e., Multimodal/Vision-Language Learning). Now, I pay the main focus on developing methods/models for multimodal human-computer interaction.
